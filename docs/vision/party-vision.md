## 🌟 1. Core Vision & Framework

The **Digital Janta** is a decentralized, democratic governance framework. The **Digital Janta Party (DJP)** is a political party built upon this framework. 

DJP operates as an **AI-enabled, digital-first political party** focusing on **local governance** (Ward Councillors, grassroot democracy) as its starting foundation before scaling to state and national levels.

### The Long-Term Goal
Transform politics from a system centered on periodic elections and personalities into a continuous collaboration platform centered on problem-solving, transparency, and measurable public impact.

---

## 📱 2. The DJPlatform App Scope

The **DJPlatform App** is the digital operating system of the movement. 

* **Open to All Citizens:** The app is open to all citizens of India. Party membership is separate and decoupled from using the app.
* **Problem Solving & Accountability:** It is a tool for citizens to log pain points on Executive, Legislative, or Judiciary (ELJ) spheres (education, infrastructure, hospitals, corruption, etc.).
* **Not for Governance or Campaigning:** The app is not a governance platform and does not directly share data with the government. It is also not a campaign management portal for official politicians. It is purely a citizen-action network.

## 👥 3. User Roles & The Leadership Loop

### 🙋 Citizen
* **Actions:** Raise Issues (I), join Discussions (D), vote in Polls (P), and Verify fixes (V). (Petitions and Research/Whitepapers to be introduced in a later phase).
* **Rewards:** Earns reputation points for all constructive actions.

### 👑 Leader
* **Eligibility:** Must cross a specific reputation threshold (Proof of Work) **AND** purchase a paid monthly subscription plan (Proof of Serious Intent).
* **Actions:** Can launch and coordinate local volunteer campaigns to resolve solvable community problems.
* **Lapse Policy:** If the monthly payment lapses, the leader enters a **14-day grace period**. During this time, they cannot create *new* campaigns, but active campaigns remain open. After 14 days, their leader actions are frozen until payment resumes.

---

## 📊 4. Reputation, Hierarchy & Dynamic Ranks

* **Dynamic Ranks:** Ranks (Area Leader → Locality Leader → Ward Leader) are automatically determined by the leader's **rolling 6-month reputation score** (recalculated periodically to ensure ranks reflect current activity).
* **Lifetime Profile Score:** A user's lifetime accumulated reputation points are preserved permanently on their profile as a badge of honor.
* **Upward Flow of Trust:** Citizens designate area leaders, area leaders choose locality leaders, and locality leaders choose ward leaders.

---

## 🏛️ 5. DJ Ceremonies (Nominee Selection)

To nominate a candidate from the DJP to represent a ward in an upcoming official public election:
1. **Shortlist:** Highest-reputation leaders in the ward are shortlisted.
2. **Townhall Presentation:** Candidates present their ward vision in a public Townhall.
3. **Selection Weighting:** The nominee is selected using a balanced, three-part formula:
   * **50%:** Public Citizen Confidence Vote
   * **25%:** Peer Leader Review
   * **25%:** DJP Governing Council Review

---

## 🤖 6. AI Categorization & Resolution

* **Solvable Problems:** AI automatically categorizes issues that citizens can solve themselves (e.g., local garbage cleanup). These are resolved via leader-led volunteer campaigns.
* **Non-Solvable Problems:** Issues requiring government action (e.g., building a flyover). Any user can launch a petition for these (petition system to be added in a later phase).
* **Double-Lock Verification:** A problem is marked as resolved only when:
  1. AI validates a "Before & After" photo comparison uploaded by the solver.
  2. Citizens in a 500-meter GPS proximity of the issue vote to confirm the resolution.
