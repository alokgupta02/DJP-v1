- Digital Janta Platform is an app, where citizen (open to all, not just member of party) can log their pain points, be it on executive, legislative, judiciary (ELJ). or any spehere of country, it could be about education, hospital, infra, corruption, society etc. Everything that concerns a citizen.

DJPlatform app is not only for DJP members, but it is open to all citizen of India. (Note: Party membership is different from using the app). This is just a tool for the people to raise their issues and get them resolved. 

this is not for governance, though we may show those problems is solved or not thrugh verification. we are NOT going to share those data with govt and track their progreess.
this is also NOT for official leaders to manage their campaigns etc.
this app is purely for citizrn to raise voice, choose their local leaders which solves problems.

# Citizen action: they can raise:
- issue (I) (garbage, pothole, footpath issue etc...)
- discussion (D) on any topic like whatever bothers them, they can have polls assciated with those discussion as well.(like a reddit post on respective community)
- polls (P)
- verify (V) the problem, solved or not etc (based on loc proximity)
- petition (later)

# how it works:

- citizen will get reputation points for each IDPV, once enough repution is build for a citizen , they can become eligible to become a leader of their area (to organize volunteer campaign, connect with other leader of same ward etc.) (note: this is like a hierarchy, everyone choose above them, like peole choose> area leader, n area leaders choose their locality leader> ward)
# AI: 
- ai will differtiate, categories problems into solveable and non solveable.
- solveable : those which can be solved by citizen themselves thru volunteering, like for a garbage issue, a leader can organize a volunteer cleaning campaign, etc)
- non solveable : those which can't be solved by people alone, need govt intervention. like flyover issue, or anything...for this, it will require a petition to be created by people, leader will represent those petition to the gov official and submit a formal complain.....
